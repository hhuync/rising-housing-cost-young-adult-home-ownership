# Rising Housing Costs and the Decline in Young Adult Homeownership

## Overview

This project explores the relationship between rising housing costs and declining homeownership rates among young adults in the United States between 1994 and 2024. Using data visualization techniques in Power BI, the dashboard examines how increasing housing prices may have contributed to reduced affordability and widening generational gaps in homeownership.

The project was completed as the final project for Data 201: Data Visualization at Dickinson College.

---

## Key Questions

* How have housing costs changed over time?
* Which age groups experienced the greatest decline in homeownership?
* Is there a visible relationship between rising housing prices and under-35 homeownership rates?
* How might these trends contribute to broader wealth inequality?

---

## Dashboard Features

### Homeownership Trends by Age Group (1994-2024)

A multi-line visualization comparing homeownership rates across age groups over time. The analysis highlights how adults under 35 consistently maintained the lowest ownership rates and experienced some of the sharpest declines.

### Housing Costs vs. Young Adult Homeownership

A combined bar and line visualization comparing housing price index growth against under-35 homeownership rates. The dashboard illustrates how affordability pressures intensified as housing prices increased substantially over the past three decades.

### 2024 Homeownership Comparison

A horizontal bar chart comparing homeownership rates by age group in 2024, emphasizing the growing ownership gap between younger and older generations.

### Key Findings Summary

The dashboard includes a written summary of major insights, connecting housing affordability challenges to long-term wealth accumulation and economic inequality.

---

## Key Insights

* Housing prices increased significantly faster than income growth over time.
* Adults under 35 consistently showed the lowest homeownership rates.
* Younger generations experienced larger barriers to entering the housing market.
* The widening ownership gap may contribute to long-term wealth inequality.

---

## Project Goals

This project aimed to combine analytical storytelling with accessible visual design to communicate a socially relevant issue through data. By blending data analytics with visual presentation principles, the dashboard was designed to make complex economic trends more understandable and engaging for viewers.
